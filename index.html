Before you submit

  • Choose the closest category.
  • Provide your purok or general area, not an exact home address.
  • Explain the issue clearly and respectfully.
  • Select urgency based on the impact of the concern.
  • Keep the reference number so you can track the request.

Understand request statuses

Pending The request has been recorded.

In Progress The request is being reviewed or handled.

Resolved The request has been marked complete.

Rejected The request was declined in this demo.

Protect your information

Do not submit passwords, financial details, government ID numbers, or sensitive personal information. This demo does not have a secure server or official case-management system.
